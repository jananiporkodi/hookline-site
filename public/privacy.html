Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This page explains what Hookline collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to see, change or delete your information.

Who we are

Hookline is a product of Mystique Gifting, a business based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, run by Janani Porkodi. When this page says "we" or "us", it means Mystique Gifting. We decide how your information is used, so under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the "data fiduciary" for it.

Questions or requests about your data: jananiporkodi@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site (waitlist, free plan, newsletter, done-for-you, or teams):

  • your name, email address and WhatsApp number, whichever you give us
  • your LinkedIn profile link and company name
  • what you tell us in the form, such as what you sell, what you want from LinkedIn, how often you want to post, your team size and the session format you prefer

When you make a free plan, we also keep the plan we wrote for you. To write it, our AI looks up the public parts of your LinkedIn profile on the web (your headline, role, company, about section and similar). It is told never to look for phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses.

When you use the Hookline WhatsApp service (once it opens):

  • the messages you send to Hookline and the drafts we send back
  • notes about your writing voice, topics and stories, so drafts sound like you
  • your posting days and time
  • a secure access key from LinkedIn that lets Hookline publish a post only after you reply "post". We never see or store your LinkedIn password.
  • payment records, such as the amount, date and payment reference. Your card, UPI or bank details are handled by our payment provider, and we never see them.

Automatically: a scrambled code made from your internet address, which changes every day, so we can limit how many free plans one person makes each day. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, which does not use cookies or track you across other sites. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • to write and send your free plan, and the drafts you asked for
  • to publish a post to LinkedIn when, and only when, you reply "post"
  • to reply to you about Hookline, done-for-you writing or team sessions you asked about
  • to send the newsletter, if you signed up for it
  • to take payments and keep the records Indian tax law requires
  • to stop misuse, such as one person making hundreds of free plans

We use your information because you gave it to us for these purposes. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawing does not undo anything we already did with your consent, and we may not be able to keep providing a service that needs the information.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to show you ads.

How long we keep it

  • Form answers and free plans: up to 2 years after we last heard from you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Hookline account, drafts and voice notes: while you use Hookline, and deleted within 30 days after you close your account.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and who we shared it with
  • correct or complete it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent
  • let someone you nominate act for you if you die or cannot act yourself

Write to jananiporkodi@gmail.com from the email or WhatsApp number you used with us, so we can check it is you. We will reply within 30 days. To stop WhatsApp messages at any time, reply STOP.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, write to our grievance officer, Janani Porkodi, at jananiporkodi@gmail.com. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Keeping it safe

Information travels over encrypted connections, access to our systems is limited to us, and LinkedIn access keys are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Hookline is for adults. Please do not use it if you are under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change matters, we will also tell Hookline users on WhatsApp or by email.
