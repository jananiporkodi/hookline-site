Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

You can stop Hookline whenever you like. Here is what happens to your money when you do.

Free month

The free first month costs nothing and needs no card, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. When it ends, we send you a payment link. If you don't pay, the drafts simply stop.

Monthly plan, ₹799 a month

  • Cancel any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or writing to jananiporkodi@gmail.com. You keep your drafts until the end of the month you paid for.
  • If you are unhappy with your first paid month, ask within 7 days of paying and we refund it in full.
  • After those 7 days, we do not refund part of a month that has already started.

6-month plan, ₹3,999

  • Ask within 7 days of paying and we refund it in full.
  • After 7 days, we refund ₹3,999 minus ₹799 for each month that has started. For example, if you cancel during your second month, you get back ₹2,401.

Done for you and team sessions

Cancellation and refunds for done-for-you writing and team sessions follow the proposal or invoice we sent you. If it says nothing, write to us and we will agree a fair answer based on the work already done.

If something goes wrong on our side

If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or did not receive drafts for days you paid for because of a problem on our side, we refund the extra or the missed days, whatever the timing.

How to ask for a refund

Write to jananiporkodi@gmail.com from the email or WhatsApp number you used with Hookline. Include the date and amount of the payment. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the way you paid, through Razorpay, within 7 working days. Your bank or UPI app may take another 5 to 7 working days to show it.

Delivery

Hookline is a digital service. Nothing is shipped. Your daily drafts start on WhatsApp within 24 hours of payment, or on the next posting day you chose.
